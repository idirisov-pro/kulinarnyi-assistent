# Approved logo source trace

The public app logo must match the logo approved for Product Radar.

The previous `icons/icon.svg` was a manually reconstructed approximation and was rejected because its geometry differed from the approved mark.

The current SVG path is traced from the approved 512×512 Product Radar logo asset, preserving the actual silhouette and relative placement of:

- the leaf;
- the mushroom;
- the rounded square ingredient with three holes;
- the three descending trajectories/arrows;
- the plate/bowl.

Do not manually redraw or simplify this mark in future releases. Any future logo change requires a newly approved master asset.
